Chrome Extension

Critter Buddy

View on Chrome Web Store ↗

A tiny animated companion that keeps you company while you browse, work, or study. Critter Buddy combines a playful character with everyday tools, including quick notes, timers, and mini games.

Coding

Making the companion interactive

The coding side brings together movement on webpages, reactions to interaction, treats, and interaction tracking. The extension also includes mini games, notes, timers, and popup controls. Settings and companion data are stored locally using Chrome storage.

Design

Giving the experience personality

The design combines an animated critter with a compact control interface. Character movement and reactions give the companion personality, while the popup organizes playful interactions and practical tools. Video-friendly behavior helps the buddy stay out of the way while watching content.
