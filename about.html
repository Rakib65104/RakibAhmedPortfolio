About me.

Hi, I’m Rakib Ahmed.

I’m an instructional design and educational technology professional with nearly three years of higher education experience supporting online course development, instructional technologies, and digital accessibility.

My work brings together LMS platforms, asynchronous course design, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), multimedia development, and accessible learning materials. I combine programming and visual design to create usable, engaging online learning experiences.

I also develop personal projects under NovaFang Labs, including Critter Buddy.
